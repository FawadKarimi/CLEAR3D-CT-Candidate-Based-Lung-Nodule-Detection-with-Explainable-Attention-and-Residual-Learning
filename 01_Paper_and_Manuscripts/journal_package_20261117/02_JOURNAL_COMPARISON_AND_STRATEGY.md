## 2. Strategic Recommendation (Agent 6 + Agent 8 + Agent 1 Reconciliation)

- **Recommended Primary Target (Best Balance of Zero Cost, JCR IF, Q1 Scopus & Acceptance Probability):** ***Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine* (JIIM, Springer Nature)** (`SCIE IF 3.1, Q1 SJR, $0 subscription, 4–5 days median first decision`).
- **Highest-Impact Realistic Zero-APC Target:** ***Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics* (CMIG, Elsevier)** (`SCIE IF 5.5, Q1, $0 subscription`).
- **Fast Zero-APC Backup:** ***Biomedical Signal Processing and Control* (BSPC, Elsevier, IF 5.7, Q1)** or ***Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing* (MBEC, Springer, IF 3.1, Q2, 6d first decision)**.
